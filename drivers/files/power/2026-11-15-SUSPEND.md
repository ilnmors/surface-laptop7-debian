## What the logs establish

Kernel PM entry explicitly says `deep` for all four intervals. Suspend statistics report success=4, fail=0, and all stage failure counters zero. There are no additional kernel suspend/resume cycles between these boundaries. This excludes a whole-night failure to suspend; it does not prove that every SoC power domain reached its lowest state.

The history does not identify a consuming device. Wi-Fi, audio/DSP, firmware or other power domains cannot be blamed from these numbers alone. The speaker no-idle workaround keeps PCM open while awake; its effect on suspend drain has not been measured with a controlled comparison. No audio resets were performed for this analysis.

## Precision and comparison

These are fuel-gauge estimates, not a power-meter measurement. Percentage steps are about 1 pp. The first and third entry samples precede suspend by 172 and 218 seconds; they include a small amount of awake/transition consumption. The other offsets are 23 and 29 seconds. Resume timestamps are rounded down by less than one second. The short 37-minute interval is particularly sensitive to quantization. The older strict-120-second baseline remains in `battery-measurement.json` unchanged.

The Nov 12 baseline was about 0.575 W / 1.13 pp/h. Today's estimate is about 0.20 W higher, but configuration, workload before sleep and boundary precision differ; this does not establish a regression or its cause.

Microsoft SleepStudy marks a sleep drain rate of at least 1%/hour as high drain. Today's approximately 1.50 pp/h exceeds that reference. Linux `deep` and Windows Modern Standby are different measurement contexts; this device has no measured Windows baseline for a direct OS comparison.

Source: [Microsoft SleepStudy thresholds](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows-hardware/design/device-experiences/modern-standby-sleepstudy), checked 2026-11-15.

## Reproduction and next diagnostic step

`measurements/2026-11-15-summary.json` preserves the PM timestamps, boundary charge samples, full capacity, suspend counters, and rate pairs used in this analysis, without account data or a battery serial.

Sleep estimate: `(charge_before - charge_after) / 100 * full_Wh / hours`.
Awake mean: `sum((W_start + W_end)/2 * seconds) / sum(seconds)` over the retained pairs.

To identify the remaining drain, compare equal, multi-hour battery-only sleep sessions while collecting before/after Qualcomm power-domain residency counters; change one device policy at a time. Preserve the working sound path, CPU defaults and 120Hz. This analysis did not start such a trial or claim a fix.
